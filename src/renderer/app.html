          Set up MyVoice

          MyVoice needs two macOS permissions. Your speech is turned into text on this Mac and is never uploaded.

          1. Microphone Hears you while you dictate, and only then.
            Checking…
          2. Accessibility Notices your fn double-tap and pastes the text at your cursor.
            Checking…

          In System Settings, open Privacy & Security > Accessibility and turn on MyVoice. If MyVoice is already on but text never appears, select it, remove it with the minus button, then add it again.

          Try it

          Does double-tapping fn open the emoji picker or Apple Dictation instead? In System Settings > Keyboard, set "Press 🌐 key to" to "Do Nothing" and give Dictation a shortcut other than pressing fn twice. You can also set your own MyVoice shortcut under Settings.
