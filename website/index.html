Why people switch

  • Keep speech local with on-device transcription.
  • Use one hotkey instead of copy/paste workflows.
  • Skip monthly fees for basic voice typing.

How it works

  1. Double-tap fn, or hold it while you talk.
  2. Speak naturally. Words appear in the overlay as you go.
  3. Pause or let go, and the text lands at your cursor.

Built for real work

  • Modes that switch by app: verbatim in Terminal, structured notes in Notes.
  • A personal dictionary for names and jargon, plus replacements like "my email".
  • Spoken commands: "new line", "new paragraph", "scratch that".
  • Searchable history, audio file transcription and eight downloadable models.

Private by design

  • Speech is turned into text on your Mac; recordings are deleted right after.
  • The only downloads are the speech models, each checked against its published checksum.
  • Update checks happen only when you ask. Optional AI cleanup only talks to a model running on your own Mac.

Support development

MyVoice is free, while most advanced dictation tools charge monthly. If this saves you time and feels worth $5, your support goes a long way and is deeply appreciated.
